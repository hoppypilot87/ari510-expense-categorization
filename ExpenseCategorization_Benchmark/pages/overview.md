# Automated Expense Categorization Challenge

## Overview

Welcome to the Automated Expense Categorization Challenge! This competition challenges you to build a machine learning model that can automatically categorize financial transactions into the correct expense category.

## Problem Description

Financial institutions and personal finance applications need to automatically categorize transactions to help users understand their spending patterns. Your task is to develop a model that can accurately predict the category of a transaction based on the provided features.

## Categories

The model should classify transactions into one of the following 12 categories:

- **Bills**: Regular bills and recurring payments
- **DiningOut**: Restaurant and food delivery expenses
- **Education**: Educational expenses, courses, books
- **Entertainment**: Movies, games, streaming services
- **Groceries**: Supermarket and grocery shopping
- **Healthcare**: Medical expenses, pharmacy, health insurance
- **Miscellaneous**: Other expenses that don't fit categories
- **PersonalCare**: Beauty, haircuts, personal grooming
- **Shopping**: Retail purchases, clothing, electronics
- **Transport**: Gas, public transit, ride-sharing
- **Travel**: Hotels, flights, vacation expenses
- **Utilities**: Electricity, water, internet, phone bills

## Evaluation

Models will be evaluated based on:
- **Accuracy**: Overall classification accuracy
- **F1 Score**: Both macro and weighted F1 scores
- **Precision & Recall**: Weighted averages across all categories
